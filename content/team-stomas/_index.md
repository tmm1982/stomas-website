## Everyone is welcome

Team STOMAS is a planned team for Relay For Life in Esbjerg, Denmark, in 2027. We welcome cancer survivors, people living with late effects, family members, friends and anyone who wants to support the cause.

You do not need a cancer diagnosis or the ability to run. Walking one lap, volunteering at the team base or making a donation all matter.

## Why we're doing this

STOMAS grew from lived experience of cancer and life after a total laryngectomy. Team STOMAS broadens that perspective to include people of different ages, diagnoses and backgrounds.

**We have three equal priorities:**

- **Fundraising:** Supporting the Danish Cancer Society (Kræftens Bekæmpelse).
- **Community:** Creating a welcoming team where everyone can take part in their own way.
- **Awareness:** Talking openly about cancer, its long-term effects and life after treatment.

## Ways to take part

**Walk or run:** Join the team once official registration opens. We aim to keep participation flexible.

**Volunteer:** Help with logistics, refreshments or the team area.

**Support or sponsor:** Individuals and businesses can contact us now. The official donation link will be added once the team has been registered.

**Want to join?** [Express your interest here](#interesse) with your contact details and a provisional T-shirt size. You must also **register separately** with the Danish Cancer Society after Team STOMAS has been created when registration opens on 15 December 2026. No health information is required.

## Sponsorship options

Our suggested levels are **Supporter DKK 500**, **Team Sponsor DKK 2,000** and **Main Supporter DKK 5,000**. Lap sponsorships or in-kind help, such as T-shirts and equipment, may also be possible. Visibility and benefits depend on individual agreements and event rules.

**Please note:** Team STOMAS is an independent team initiative, not the Relay For Life organiser. Fundraising contributions go to the Danish Cancer Society through official channels and remain separate from STOMAS business finances.
