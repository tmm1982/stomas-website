WORK WITH STOMAS

Lived experience brings another perspective.

STOMAS is built on personal experience of life after a total laryngectomy. The aim is to turn patient experience into practical input for products, communication, teaching and everyday interactions.

🎥

Why STOMAS?

I live the life we are talking about.

I know what it is like to learn to speak again, breathe through my neck, manage medical devices, return to work and make everyday life work in a different way.

This experience can add a personal perspective on products, communication and care pathways alongside clinical data.

A good collaboration should be valuable to patients, the partner and STOMAS.
