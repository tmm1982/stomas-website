The story behind STOMAS

My name is Thomas.
I live without a voice box.

My entire larynx was removed because of cancer. The operation saved my life and changed the way I speak, breathe and live.

Since then, I have discovered how little many people know about life after laryngectomy. I want to help change that.

I share what life is actually like, with the difficult parts, the unexpected parts and the things we can laugh about.
